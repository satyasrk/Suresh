Company Overview

Built for Dependability, Scaled for the Modern Logistics Landscape

Our business was built around a simple objective: to help companies manage their delivery requirements through reliable fleet capacity, professional delivery operations, and consistent service.

Over the years, we have developed our capabilities through investment in our own vehicles, fleet infrastructure, operational teams, and long-term business partnerships.

Today, Ultra Miles operates with 200+ owned vehicles supported by our own garage, providing us with greater control over fleet maintenance, vehicle availability, and operational readiness.

Our experience working with leading delivery, e-commerce, food, retail, and technology platforms has helped us develop the operational capabilities required to manage demanding delivery environments.
